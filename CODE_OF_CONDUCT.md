# Community conduct

StackRinse welcomes contributors of every background and experience level. Critique the work with specific evidence, credit others, and respect requests for privacy. Harassment, discriminatory language, intimidation and publication of private information are unacceptable.

Report a concern privately to the repository owner through an available private contact channel on their GitHub profile. To request a private channel publicly, omit names and incident details. Maintainers may remove content, restrict participation or block contributors to protect participants. Maintainers reviewing a report should limit disclosure to the people needed to resolve it and recuse themselves when directly involved.
